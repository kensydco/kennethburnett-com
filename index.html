01 / The perspective

Technology is most valuable when it changes what a business can do.

My work has always lived where business strategy, operational reality and technology meet.

The discipline of enterprise technology. The perspective of an owner. The possibilities of applied AI.

I began in industrial automation, then moved to Arizona to join Sequencia, a venture-backed industrial technology startup. Later roles took me through hospital systems, financial services and nonprofit technology before I began building businesses of my own.

I’ve worked inside large organizations and small startups, helped launch new operating businesses, and operated franchises. Each setting taught me something different about how decisions get made and how work actually gets done.

That experience informs my AI work today: understand the people, the process and the economics before deciding what to automate.
